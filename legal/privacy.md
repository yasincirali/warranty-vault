# Coverline Privacy Policy

Coverline is called Rafım in Turkish.

Version 1.1 · 23 October 2026 · [Türkçe](gizlilik.md)

1. **Data we collect:** None. The products, photos, receipts, documents and reminders you add are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to us or to any third party.
2. **Photos:** Lifting a product off its background and guessing its kind happen on your phone, with Apple's own model. The photo never leaves the device.
3. **Notifications:** Reminders before a right ends are scheduled by your phone's own notification system; no server is involved.
4. **Backup:** You create the backup file and decide where it is saved. It is not encrypted; keep it somewhere safe.
5. **Ads, tracking, analytics:** None.
6. **Deletion:** Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your phone.
7. **Legal note:** The rights shown in the app are general information, not legal advice.
8. **Contact:** For questions or requests: https://github.com/yasincirali/warranty-vault/issues

If this policy changes, the version number goes up and the change is written on this page.
