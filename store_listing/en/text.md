# App Store text (EN)

- **Name (≤30):** Coverline: Warranty & Rights
- **Subtitle (≤30):** Receipts, warranties, returns
- **Keywords (≤100):** receipt,invoice,return,refund,consumer,reminder,vault,purchase,proof,tracker,guarantee,repair,expiry
  (Words already in the name, "warranty" and "rights", are indexed by Apple; repeating them wastes space.)
- **Privacy policy URL:** https://github.com/yasincirali/warranty-vault/blob/main/legal/privacy.md

## Promotional text
Everything you bought, on one shelf. Snap a product and Coverline lifts it off the background. Add the date and country to see when your return window, legal rights and warranty end, on one timeline. Reminders before they run out. Receipts stay next to the product, and your data stays on your phone.

The rights shown in the app are general information, not legal advice.
